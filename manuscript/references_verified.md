## Notes from Weber et al. (2019), read from the arXiv PDF
- 49 time steps; nodes in different time steps are not connected.
- 70:30 temporal split: first 34 time steps for training, remaining for test.
- Random Forest (scikit-learn) with 50 estimators and 50 max features.
- "Local features" = the first 94 features (these include the time step); 72 aggregated features.
- A dark market closes at time step 43; all methods perform poorly after it.
- Table 1 is garbled in text extraction: do not quote its numbers without checking the PDF visually.

## Notes from Lorenz et al. (2020), read from the author PDF (arXiv:2005.14635)
- Tested LOF, KNN, PCA, OC-SVM, CBLOF, ABOD and Isolation Forest (PyOD defaults) on Elliptic.
- All far below a Random Forest baseline at every contamination level; "illicit cases are indeed not outlying";
  conclude anomaly detection is ineffective for this task; active learning with ~5% of labels matches the supervised baseline.
